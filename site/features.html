Shipped

Here today

Read, write, search and moderate through AT Protocol services that expose the relevant lexicons. The Following feed stays chronological; everything else is an explicit destination.

AT Protocol OAuth
Sign in with a handle, DID or PDS URL. No password field, DPoP-bound tokens.
Chronological Following
Newest first, paginated, pull-to-refresh. No ranking or injected posts.
Threads and replies
Ancestor chains, capped nested branches and a reply composer with correct roots. Every view is labelled as a thread, and each post says whether it is earlier in the conversation, the one you opened, or a reply.
Profiles
Posts, Replies, Media, Videos and Likes tabs, followers, following, known followers and per-post engagement lists, plus likeable feeds and labelers with a real liked-by list, verification badges, label warnings, pinned posts and authored lists, feeds and starter packs.
Profile editing
Name, bio, pronouns, links, avatar and banner.
Search
Top, Latest, People, Feeds and Starter Packs, with filters, local history and account-scoped post search from any profile.
Notifications
Tabs, grouped activity, seen state, unread count and per-category preferences. A profile bell opts in to an account's posts and replies, with a management list in settings.
Live status
Go live from your own profile with a published app.bsky.actor.status record, choose a link and duration, and see a LIVE badge on active profiles.
Private bookmarks
Bluesky saved posts, removable in place from the Saved tab.
Photo posts
Up to ten images, per-image alt text, compression, retries and a full-screen viewer.
Video and GIF posts
MP4 and animated GIF uploads with visible processing states, cancellable waits, optional WebVTT captions and Bluesky's muted GIF presentation in the feed.
Post options
Content warnings from Bluesky's self-label vocabulary, up to three post languages, and reply and quote controls written to real threadgate and postgate records. Both composers enforce the 300-grapheme and 3,000-byte text caps with a live counter (issue #83 ↗). Edit interaction settings on any post you authored.
Rich text
Mentions, links and hashtags are written as real rich-text facets. Mention handles are resolved to DIDs; an unresolvable handle stays plain text rather than being stored with an empty DID. The composer suggests matching accounts as an @handle is typed (issue #79 ↗), and thread replies write the same facets and offer the same suggestions (issue #81 ↗).
Drafts
An unfinished post is kept on your device with a restore-or-discard prompt, and saved drafts live in your private Bluesky stash through the real app.bsky.draft RPCs. Reopen, update, delete and clear-on-publish all work; photos, videos and quoted posts need adding again.
Custom feeds
Discover, save, pin and reorder generators, and pin a curation list’s feed beside them (issue #85 ↗). Switch feed on Home puts Following first and any saved feed or list feed one tap away.
Lists
Create and edit curation and moderation lists, manage members, read list feeds, subscribe or block.
Moderation
Report posts, accounts and lists; block and mute; label warnings before content; labeler filters, muted words and an inbox for reports and appeals. Sent reports leave a local-only receipt on your device (issue #51 ↗).
Post actions
Copy link, copy text, share sheet, send a post via direct message to a recent chat or a person, translate with a show-original toggle, and delete your own posts.
Appearance
System/Light/Dark, post text size, four Facebook-era presets and nine accents. Native builds can also switch between the Balla Light and Dark app icons; the choice is read back from your device and needs a rebuilt app to see a new icon.
Content and media
Choose once whether links open in Balla's in-app browser or your system browser, and turn off video autoplay. External links share one opener; mailto, tel and SMS links always stay with the system handler.
Preference editors
Alt-text requirement and larger description badges, primary and content languages, Following filters for replies/reposts/quotes, and thread reply order. Feed and thread values are saved through Bluesky's app.bsky.actor preferences; the rest stay on your device.
Account and security
Real birthday and automation-label writes, logged-out visibility self-label, activity privacy, per-account activity notifications, notification preferences and non-secret OAuth session facts. Email, password, 2FA, app passwords and account lifecycle link to your provider; export, deactivation and deletion guidance links to your PDS controls.
Web client
Available at /app with popup OAuth that returns to the same tab. Every screen has its own address under /app, so threads and profiles can be linked and browser back works.
Reliability and truthful failures
Bounded retries on reads, Retry-After waits, rollback on failed likes, reposts and saves, and visible errors for posts, replies, deletes and reports (issue #34 ↗).
Accessibility pass
Labelled actions and dialogs, trapped focus and visible keyboard rings, text that scales without clipping, reduced motion, and 44pt controls with no gesture-only actions (issue #35 ↗).
AppView health check
Advanced → AppView reads the grant-bound AppView's public did:web document and one public profile canary, reporting HTTP status and latency from this device. It does not measure sync lag, offer failover or switch providers; that needs a new authorisation (issue #99 ↗, partial).
Network backend seam
Feed, thread, notification, profile and search reads use one typed contract and the registered AT Protocol adapter. Session, compose/reply, media and moderation calls remain direct, so this is not a second backend or a completed migration (issue #98 ↗).

What never ships

These are product rules, not preferences. They are why some popular features will not appear in Balla.

Chronological or nothing

Ranking, suggestions and inserted content belong in another app. Following stays newest first.

Visible buttons only

No swipe-only or long-press-only actions. Every control is tappable, labelled and at least 44pt.

Protocol truth

Shapes follow the app.bsky lexicons, not one convenient response. Claims describe the code.

A wall, not a feed machine.

  • × No engagement ranking or A/B tests on your feed
  • × No ads, analytics or tracking SDKs
  • × No “people you may know” or reaction palette
  • ✓ Open source, AGPL-3.0, with public issues
