Shipped

Here today

Read, write, search and moderate on any AT Protocol service host. The Following feed stays chronological; everything else is an explicit destination.

  • AT Protocol OAuthSign in with a handle, DID or PDS URL. No password field, DPoP-bound tokens.
  • Chronological FollowingNewest first, paginated, pull-to-refresh. No ranking or injected posts.
  • Threads and repliesAncestor chains, capped nested branches and a reply composer with correct roots.
  • ProfilesPosts, followers, following, known followers and per-post engagement lists.
  • Profile editingName, bio, pronouns, links, avatar and banner.
  • SearchTop, Latest, People, Feeds and Starter Packs, with filters and local history.
  • NotificationsTabs, grouped activity, seen state, unread count and per-category preferences.
  • Private bookmarksBluesky saved posts, removable in place from the Saved tab.
  • Photo postsUp to ten images, per-image alt text, compression, retries and a full-screen viewer.
  • Custom feedsDiscover, save, pin, reorder and read generator feeds in their own tabs.
  • ListsCreate and edit curation and moderation lists, manage members, read list feeds, subscribe or block.
  • ModerationReport, block and mute; label warnings before content; labeler filters, muted words and an inbox for reports and appeals.
  • Post actionsCopy link, copy text, share sheet, translate with a show-original toggle, and delete your own posts.
  • AppearanceFour researched Facebook-era presets, nine accents, system light and dark.
  • Account guidanceExport, deactivation and deletion routes that link to your PDS controls.
  • Web clientAvailable at /app with popup OAuth that returns to the same tab.
  • Reliability and truthful failuresBounded retries on reads, Retry-After waits, rollback on failed likes, reposts and saves, and visible errors for posts, replies, deletes and reports (issue #34 ↗).

How we decide

What never ships

These are product rules, not preferences. They are why some popular features will not appear in Balla.

Chronological or nothing

Ranking, suggestions and inserted content belong in another app. Following stays newest first.

Visible buttons only

No swipe-only or long-press-only actions. Every control is tappable, labelled and at least 44pt.

Protocol truth

Shapes follow the app.bsky lexicons, not one convenient response. Claims describe the code.

The line we hold

A wall, not a feed machine.

  • × No engagement ranking or A/B tests on your feed
  • × No ads, analytics or tracking SDKs
  • × No “people you may know” or reaction palette
  • ✓ Open source, AGPL-3.0, with public issues
