Why Balla exists

Facebook’s reading experience was never the problem.

Balla exists because of my mum. She was comfortable with Facebook and found Bluesky’s interface confusing; the posts were there, but the shape of the app was not.

Facebook’s warm grey canvas, cards from people you actually know and a newest-first feed worked. The dark patterns wrapped around them did not. Balla keeps the reading experience and refuses the rest.

That is a design reference, not an affiliation. Balla is independent software for Bluesky; it is not a Meta or Bluesky product, and neither company endorses it.

What’s inside

A familiar wall.
Bluesky underneath.

01

Newest first. Always.

The Following feed is chronological by design. No ranking, no injected content and no engagement experiment.

02

People and lists

Public profiles, follow and unfollow, curated lists with their own feeds, threads and replies. The social graph is yours.

03

Search when you ask

Five result tabs, structured filters and local history. Find people and posts explicitly, never as a suggestion on every surface.

04

Quiet saves and feeds

Private Bluesky bookmarks, saved custom feeds behind a visible switcher, and moderation lists you can subscribe to or block.

05

Safety in the open

Report, block and mute controls, label warnings before content, and a moderation inbox for your own reports and appeals.

06

Readable by design

Four researched Facebook-era presets, nine accent colours, light and dark palettes, image alt text and large touch targets.
