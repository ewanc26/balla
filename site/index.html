Why Balla exists

Facebook’s reading experience was never the problem.

Balla exists because of my mum. She was comfortable with Facebook and found Bluesky’s interface confusing; the posts were there, but the shape of the app was not.

Facebook’s warm grey canvas, cards from people you actually know and a newest-first feed worked. The dark patterns wrapped around them did not. Balla keeps the reading experience and refuses the rest.

That is a design reference, not an affiliation. Balla is independent software for Bluesky; it is not a Meta or Bluesky product, and neither company endorses it.

What’s inside

A familiar wall.
Bluesky underneath.

01

Newest first. Always.

The Following feed is chronological by design. There is no ranking, no injected content and no engagement experiment.

02

People and lists, not a marketplace

Public profiles, follow and unfollow, curated lists with their own feeds, threads and replies. The social graph is yours, not an ad targeting product.

03

Search when you ask

Find people and posts across Bluesky explicitly. No suggested accounts on every surface, no attention hijacking.

04

Lists and quiet saves

Curate people into lists with their own feed, save and pin custom feeds, and keep Bluesky bookmarks private. Moderation lists can be subscribed to or blocked.

05

Readable by design

Four researched Facebook-era presets and nine solid accent colours, with light and dark palettes. Era styling is a visual reference only.

06

AT Protocol all the way down

Your account stays on Bluesky or whatever PDS you use. Balla is an ordinary client, not a new silo.
