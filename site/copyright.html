Content shown in Balla

Posts, images, profiles and other records belong to the account that published them. They live on that account’s PDS and are shown through Bluesky’s AppView. Balla does not keep a separate copy on a Balla server and cannot delete another account’s records.

Reporting content

Use the report option on the post or profile. The report goes to Bluesky’s moderation labeler. For content hosted by a particular PDS, you may also need to contact that host directly.

Brand assets

Balla ships its own wall mark. No Facebook or Bluesky logos, wordmarks or other brand assets are used as Balla’s branding. Product names appear only to identify the network, protocol and design period being referenced.

Balla’s own code and site

For a copyright concern about Balla’s source, branding or this site, open an issue at github.com/ewanc26/balla/issues with the URL, the work concerned and enough detail to identify it. Do not include private personal information in a public issue.

Balla is independent software and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Bluesky Social PBC or Meta Platforms, Inc. Facebook-era styling is a design reference only; no Facebook logos, wordmarks or other brand assets are used. This is a plain-language project policy, not legal advice.
