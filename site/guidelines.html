Community guidelines

Be a person.
Leave room for others.

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Treat people with respect

Harassment, threats, targeted abuse, hate and evading someone’s block are not acceptable. Do not use Balla to coordinate pile-ons or to keep contacting someone who has asked you to stop.

Label sensitive content

Use content labels honestly, especially for adult, sexual or graphic media. Do not use a warning label as a joke or to hide illegal content.

Do not spam or manipulate

No bulk posting, follow farming, deceptive engagement, malware or scams. Do not scrape or automate interactions at a scale that burdens the services.

Report rather than repost

If content breaks the rules, report it through the post or profile menu. Reposting it to a wider audience can cause more harm. Use mute when you need space and block when you need a firm boundary.

How reports are handled

When you report a post or profile in Balla, the report is sent to Bluesky’s moderation labeler through the AT Protocol. Balla does not host the content or employ moderators, so the labeler decides what action, if any, follows. Keep any reference the reporting flow gives you if you need to follow up with that service.

For a problem with Balla itself — a bug, an accessibility barrier, a policy concern or a security issue — use the issue tracker, which is monitored by the maintainer. For anything that should not be public, use GitHub’s private advisory form. Please do not include personal data in a public issue.

Who enforces this

Balla does not host posts and does not employ moderators. Bluesky and the labelers that supply moderation labels decide what happens to reported content. Your PDS may have its own rules and enforcement process.

Contact

For project issues, use the issue tracker. For a private security or privacy report, use GitHub’s advisory form.
